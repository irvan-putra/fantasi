# Redesign kota viewer: daylight look + autoplay cinematic flythrough

You are rewriting ONE FILE: site/index.html of the kota project (current version is below, in full). It is a MapLibre GL 3.6.2 viewer for a 1:1 3D map of Green Pramuka City, Jakarta, served as static files on GitHub Pages. Tiles: buildings (fill-extrusion, height property `h`), roads (LineStrings, properties kind=highway/waterway/railway, tier 1-3, rid), plus a NEW static file tiles/green.json (one GeoJSON FeatureCollection: polygons with kind=green (parks/grass/cemeteries/woods), polygons kind=water, and Points kind=tree). The viewer already has: viewport tile loading with progress bar + counter, coarse preview, poster first-paint, a boot that captures the map-load promise at construction (KEEP THIS EXACT PATTERN - attaching the load listener after an await caused a shipped hang), a simulated-traffic engine (cars + people as circle layers on real road geometry), a Tour button, and an honesty note on the start card.

THE GOAL: the current look is a dark flat-grey night theme and the owner compared it with its inspiration - Gabriel Chua's 1:1 Singapore flythrough video (day flyovers with motion blur, green parks, teal water, terracotta roofs, ground-level life, dusk and night scenes, title cards at start and end) - and called ours "still bad". Make it feel like that video, as far as static tiles + MapLibre on a mid-range phone allow.

REQUIREMENTS:
1. DAYLIGHT THEME. Sky-blue page/background color (#a9c9e8 to #cfe3f7 range), MapLibre fog for aerial haze (setFog, subtle, horizon blend), ground between buildings a warm light urban tone (#b8b2a4 to #c9c4b4 range - NOT green everywhere; green comes from the parks layer).
2. BUILDINGS WITH ROOF VARIETY. Color each building by height band AND a per-feature hash (use the feature's existing `h` plus a stable hash of its geometry or index to pick from 3-4 roof tones per band): low-rise (<=10m) warm terracotta/tan mix (#c96f4a, #d98e5f, #c4835a, #e0a06b), mid (10-40m) light concrete/stone mix (#cfc8bd, #bfc4cc, #d8cfc0), tall (>40m) glassy blue-grey mix (#8fa8c8, #9fb6d6, #7e96bc). Flat colors, NO vertical gradient (that read as night). Opacity 1. Keep the existing fade-in-by-zoom behavior.
3. PARKS + WATER from tiles/green.json: fill green areas #79a861 (parks) with tree points as darker green circles (#4e7d3f, radius by zoom ~1-3px, opacity 0.85); water fill #6fa8cf. Load it ONCE at boot (single fetch, ~257KB, treat failure as non-fatal). Render green/water UNDER buildings and roads; trees UNDER buildings too (they poke out around them).
4. ROADS DAY STYLE: casings: major roads light asphalt #a9a49a with slightly darker edge, mid #b7b2a8, minor #c4bfb4; waterways as #6fa8cf lines (width 2-6 by zoom) IN ADDITION to the water polygons; railway #8a8494 dashed. Remove the night glow layers.
5. AUTOPLAY CINEMATIC (the headline feature). No click needed: keep the start card as the title card (restyle it for daylight: white/soft card, dark text), auto-dismiss it ~3s after the map is interactive, then run a ~20-25s chained flight with map.flyTo/easeTo: (a) start high zoom ~13.2 low pitch, (b) dive toward the towers zoom ~15.5 pitch ~60, (c) a LOW run along the toll road zoom ~16.6 pitch ~70 bearing following the road, (d) rise + pull back to an overview zoom ~14.6, then settle into the existing slow orbit. Use the existing VIEWS coordinates as anchors and add a toll-road run view near [106.8790, -6.1860]. Any user gesture (existing releaseCamera) interrupts the cinematic and hands over control. Honor prefers-reduced-motion (skip cinematic, keep card until clicked). Keep the Tour button as a replay of this cinematic.
6. BUSES: add 3 slower, bigger, TransJakarta-red (#d63c2f) agents restricted to tier-3 roads in the existing traffic engine. Keep cars warm-amber and people light blue-grey but restyle for daylight visibility (darker outlines ok).
7. KEEP WORKING: tile loader, progress bar, counter, poster (will be re-baked), tile eviction, coarse preview, the captured-mapReady boot pattern, agents engine, honesty notes on the card ("Building heights are estimates..." and "Cars and people are simulated."; add "Parks and water from OpenStreetMap."), the inspiration credit line, and the counter. Do not add any new external dependency. Single file, no build step. Phone performance: total new layers <= 6; the traffic engine stays at 15 Hz.
8. TITLE CARDS: during the cinematic show a small floating location label (reuse the existing tour label element) naming each stop, like the video's title beats.
